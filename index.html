Studying entropy.

I'm a fourth-year PhD candidate at Caltech working on two things that happen to be very far apart: the thermodynamics of high-entropy alloys and building a miniaturized Mössbauer spectrometer for studying mineralogy on the surface of Mars. In between, I write code, document every latte art attempt I make, and run student organizations at Caltech.
