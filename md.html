Melt a crystal

A small molecular dynamics simulation written for this site. Drag the temperature and watch.

All projects

A few hundred atoms that attract at a distance and repel up close, and nothing else. Heat them and the crystal melts. Cool them and it freezes again, usually with mistakes. This runs live in your browser.

toy modelPush the temperature past about 0.7 and most atoms lose their crystal-like surroundings. The plot tracks temperature (gold) and the fraction of crystal-like atoms (green). Quench drops the temperature at once; the crystal comes back, but with defects and grain boundaries. Switch to five elements of different sizes and even the cold crystal is strained, which is the size-mismatch effect in high-entropy alloys.

What is happening

Every atom feels a Lennard-Jones pull from its neighbors. Each frame the code adds up those forces, moves every atom a short step with velocity Verlet, and gently rescales speeds toward the temperature you set. Units are reduced, so one atom's size and bond energy both equal 1. The box wraps around at the edges and everything is 2-D, so treat the numbers as qualitative.
