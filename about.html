Background

I'm a fourth-year PhD student at the California Institute of Technology working in materials science. My research spans two pretty different problems: understanding the thermodynamic role of entropy in high-entropy alloys, and engineering a miniaturized Mössbauer spectrometer for Mars surface mineralogy.

The second is the kind of project where you spend years making something smaller and more robust so that, one day, it might answer a question about another planet.

Outside the lab, I write code, lead things, and make latte art. I've documented every latte attempt I've made. It's a data set now.
